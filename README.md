📚 Management Information Systems (MIS) – BBA Notes

Welcome to the Management Information Systems (MIS) learning repository.

This repository contains complete, student-friendly and examination-oriented notes for BBA students.

The objective is simple:

Understand MIS not as "computer knowledge", but as the way businesses use information and technology to solve problems and make better decisions.
🎓 Course Learning Journey
BUSINESS ACTIVITIES
        ->DATA->INFORMATION->INFORMATION SYSTEMS->MIS->MANAGERIAL DECISIONS-> BUSINESS ACTION->BETTER RESULTS

👨‍🏫 Faculty Note

The objective of these notes is not simply to help you pass the examination.

The objective is to help you think like a future manager:

What information does my business need?

Where does that information come from?

How is it processed?

Who needs it?

What decision can it support?

What technology can help?

What risks and ethical issues should I consider?
