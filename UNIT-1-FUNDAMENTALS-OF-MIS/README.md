## 🎯 What You Should Be Able to Do After This Unit

By the end of Unit 1, you should be able to:

* Explain MIS in simple terms.
* Distinguish between data and information.
* Explain major components of information systems.
* Identify different types of information systems.
* Explain the dimensions of information systems.
* Describe the benefits of MIS.
* Explain IT infrastructure.
* Describe the evolution of IT infrastructure.
* Identify major IT infrastructure components.
* Explain modern approaches to system building.

---

### 👨‍🏫 Learning Tip

Do not try to memorize MIS definitions first.

Instead, ask:

> **What business problem is this system solving?**

That question will help you understand the entire subject.
