# 🔥 Let's Start With a Question

Imagine a large company such as a supermarket, bank, university, or e-commerce company.

Different people in the organization need different kinds of information.

For example:

- A cashier needs information about today's transactions.
- A department manager needs a sales summary.
- A senior manager wants to know whether the business is growing.
- A top executive wants to know whether the company is moving toward its long-term goals.

### 🤔 Question

**Should all these people receive exactly the same information?**

Obviously, **NO**.

The information required depends on:

- Who is using it?
- What decision are they making?
- How frequently do they need it?
- How detailed should it be?
- At what level of management do they work?

This is why organizations use **different types of information systems**.

# 1. What Are Types of MIS?

An organization does not use one single system for every activity.

Different systems are designed to support different:

- Business activities
- Users
- Management levels
- Decisions
- Information requirements

Therefore, information systems can be classified according to the **purpose they serve and the users they support**.
